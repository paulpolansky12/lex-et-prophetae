Notes

Dates are approximate. Solomon’s follow the Thiele chronology (970–931), and David’s forty years run back from there. Saul’s are the least certain: the Hebrew of 1 Samuel 13:1, which should give his age and reign, is damaged, and the forty years of Acts 13:21 is the usual figure.

Older Catholic Bibles (Douay-Rheims) call 1–2 Samuel “1–2 Kings,” 1 Kings “3 Kings,” and 1 Chronicles “1 Paralipomenon,” and spell many names differently, such as Heli for Eli or Bethsabee for Bathsheba. Those spellings are in each person’s details.

Chronicles starts its story at Saul’s death and leaves out David’s outlaw years, Bathsheba and Uriah, and Absalom’s revolt. The chapter picker for 1 Chronicles lights up only the people it actually names.
